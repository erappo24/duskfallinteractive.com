Account Management

Request Account Deletion

If you would like to delete your Dungeon Alone account and all associated data, follow the steps below. This process is permanent and cannot be undone.

Account deletion is permanent. All your characters, campaigns, game history, and progress will be deleted and cannot be recovered.

What Gets Deleted

  • Your account and login credentials
  • All characters and their stats, inventory, and spells
  • All campaigns and game session history
  • Your character portrait images
  • Any unused credits

What Gets Retained

  • Transaction records may be retained for up to 90 days for legal and accounting purposes
  • No gameplay data is retained after the 90-day period

How to Request Deletion

  1. Send an email to contact@duskfallinteractive.com from the email address associated with your account
  2. Use the subject line: Account Deletion Request — Dungeon Alone
  3. Include your in-game username or character name so we can locate your account
  4. We will confirm your request within 48 hours and complete the deletion within 7 days

Contact

Questions about your data? Reach us at contact@duskfallinteractive.com
